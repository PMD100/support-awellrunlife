## Important caveat

Every listing is therefore written with `cost: unknown`, a schedule of "Contact the organization for current meeting times", and `verification_status: needs_review`. They will display with the *Unconfirmed — call ahead* badge and **cannot count toward a metro's publish threshold** until someone confirms the details.

That is the correct outcome, not a shortfall. Inventing a meeting time we were never given is the one error this project cannot afford.
