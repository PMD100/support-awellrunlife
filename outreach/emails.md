## Email 1 — to organizations already listed

**This is the one to send first.** There are 29 of them, they are already in the directory,
and the email is about the accuracy of their own information rather than about us. That is
why it gets opened and answered.

Every reply does three things at once: corrects the data, upgrades the listing from
*Unconfirmed* to *Confirmed by the organization*, and puts you in conversation with a
bereavement coordinator — the person who hands physical resources to grieving families
every week.

**Subject:** Your grief support groups are listed on a free directory — did we get it right?

---

Hello,

I've listed [ORGANIZATION]'s grief support groups in a free directory of bereavement
support across the country, and I wanted you to see it before anyone else does:

**https://awellrunlifegear.com/pages/support-center-sponsored-by-a-well-run-life**

Would you take sixty seconds to check that what we've said about your groups is right?
Meeting times change, groups pause, and the last thing I want is to send a grieving
person somewhere on a Tuesday when nothing is happening. Anything you tell me gets
fixed within 72 hours.

If you'd rather not be listed at all, say the word and I'll remove you the same day.
No follow-up, no persuasion.

A word about who I am, because you should know: I run A Well Run Life, a small business
in Chandler, Arizona that makes handmade bronze memorial charms. This directory is free
and always will be — no advertising, nothing behind an email signup, and placement is
never for sale. My business simply pays for it. I'd rather tell you that plainly than
have you find out later and wonder.

Thank you for the work you do.

directory genuinely worth finding lives with these organizations.

## Sending notes

**Send Email 1 first**, and send it one at a time rather than as a blast. A bereavement
coordinator can tell the difference, and this is the audience where that matters most.
Twenty-nine emails is maybe an hour.

**Do not pitch the charms.** Not in the first email, not in the reply. The funding
disclosure is there for honesty, not as a soft sell. If a wholesale conversation ever
happens, it will be because you were useful first — and it will be them raising it.

**For Email 2, start with MISS Foundation.** They're Arizona-founded, which makes it a
local call rather than a cold one, and it's the cheapest place to learn how these
conversations go before writing to the larger organizations.

**When someone replies:**

| Reply | What to do |
|---|---|
| Corrections | Fix within 72 hours, mark `org_confirmed` |
| "Looks right" | Mark `org_confirmed` — this is the goal |
| Sends a data file | Import it; this is the best possible outcome |
| "Please remove us" | Remove same day, mark `declined`, never revisit |
| Silence after 3 weeks | One short follow-up, then leave it |
